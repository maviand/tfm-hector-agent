PN-SDQ-2026-110101
November 1, 2026
The insured vehicle was traveling on Autopista Las Américas when a construction vehicle ahead suddenly lost some debris. To
avoid impact with the debris, the driver swerved sharply to the left, causing the vehicle to scrape against the concrete guardrail
along the median. The impact primarily affected the entire left side of the vehicle, including the front fender, driver's door, and
rear door. The driver was uninjured. The National Police (Policía Nacional) was called to the scene and filed a report. The
vehicle was driven to Taller Automotriz del Este.
