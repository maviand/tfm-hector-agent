How It Works

From unstructured chaos to strictly typed JSON objects in seconds.

1. Document Ingestion

Users upload raw insurance claim documents (PDFs, images, or scanned forms). Hector handles mixed multimodal contexts seamlessly.

2. Vision-Language Processing

Gemini 2.5 Flash analyzes the text alongside visual damage in photographs, assessing severity and identifying judicial likelihood.

3. Structured Extraction

The unstructured data is mapped perfectly into a strict Pydantic schema, ensuring type safety and immediate database readiness.

Enterprise Capabilities

Built for the complex demands of the modern insurance industry.

Intelligent Parsing

Seamlessly process PDFs and scanned images, converting unstructured claim documents into structured text.

Gemini 2.5 Flash Engine

Leverages Google's state-of-the-art vision-language models to extract complex entities and understand context.

Damage Assessment

Analyzes accident photographs to describe visible damage and estimate severity automatically.

Litigation Prediction

Flags RC (Responsabilidad Civil) claims with high probabilities of judicialization, allowing early legal intervention.

Type-Safe Validation

Built on Pydantic to ensure all extracted data strictly adheres to database constraints before saving.

Synthetic Data Generation

Includes built-in pipelines to generate thousands of realistic, synthetic insurance claims for robust testing.
